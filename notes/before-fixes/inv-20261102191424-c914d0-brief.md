> Customers are seeing errors at checkout and the site feels slow. Started maybe 15–20 min ago.

## Summary
CFG-502 (inventory DB_POOL_MAX 20->80 at 13:52) lifted the potential connection budget on orders-db to 610 vs max 400. inventory holds ~318 connections (LOG-076, LOG-121), starving orders-service ("too many clients"), which causes checkout errors. High confidence, not human-confirmed.

**Overall confidence:** 93%
