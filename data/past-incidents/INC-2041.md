# INC-2041: Orders failing, orders-db connection exhaustion

**Date:** 2026-06-11 · **Severity:** SEV-2 · **Duration:** 52 min
**Services affected:** checkout-service, orders-service, inventory-service

## Summary
reporting-service raised its DB pool from 15 to 100 to speed up a quarterly report. Its 2 replicas opened up to 200 connections to orders-db, pushing total connections to the 400 limit. orders-service and inventory-service could not acquire connections, causing checkout 500s (~9%) and p99 latency above 4s.

## Timeline
- 10:02 reporting-service pool change via config-service (no deploy)
- 10:09 orders-db connections at 97%
- 10:11 checkout 5xx alert
- 10:25 responders roll back an unrelated search-service deploy (no effect)
- 10:41 DBRE runs `pg_stat_activity`, finds reporting_svc holding 196 connections
- 10:44 pool reverted, recovery by 10:54

## What went well
- `pg_stat_activity` identified the culprit in one query.

## What went poorly
- 30 minutes lost investigating the most recent *deploy* rather than recent *config changes*.
- Config-service changes weren't visible on the deploy dashboard.

- [ ] Add PgBouncer in front of orders-db (**open**)
- [ ] Config-service guardrail on pool sizes (**open**)
