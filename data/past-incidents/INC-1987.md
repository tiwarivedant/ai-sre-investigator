# INC-1987: Checkout 500s after payments deploy

**Date:** 2026-03-19 · **Severity:** SEV-2 · **Duration:** 24 min
**Services affected:** checkout-service, payments-service

## Summary
payments-service v2.9.0 shipped a serialization bug for a new card-network field. ~12% of authorize calls returned 500, which checkout surfaced as place-order failures.

## Key signals
- payments-service's **own** error rate jumped from 0.2% to 12% within 2 minutes of the 100% rollout.
- checkout errors all read `payments authorize failed: 500`.
- orders-db and inventory metrics were normal.

## Resolution
Rolled back to v2.8.3; errors cleared within 3 minutes.

- [x] Add canary analysis to payments deploys (canary now blocks on error-rate regression)
